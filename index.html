The Legacy Begins

A Legacy of Empowerment

From a Sanctuary for Women to a Global Powerhouse

Inspired by the global feminist movement of the late 1950s, our founder Marie established an atelier designed as a sanctuary where women could flourish. By 1971, Marie’s dedication, paired with Amin’s strategic vision, secured our first major international milestone: 60,000 pieces exported to Poland.

Technological leadership is in our DNA. In 1972, we introduced the first automatic embroidery machine to the Middle East. Under the guidance of Boulos Massoud and his wife Ivy Gobran, the workshop transitioned into the state-of-the-art factory VIVA is today.

Led by Mark Boulos, VIVA now operates from our New Cairo facility, proving that heritage and high-tech automation are the perfect blend for luxury fashion.
